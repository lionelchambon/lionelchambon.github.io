[**Bundesbank Internship Project**](/files/Chambon_BundesbankProject_EN.pdf) As an intern at the DG Economics of the Bundesbank, I researched current dynamics of private consumption in the Euro area. To do so, I estimated an error-correction model (ECM) using macro time-series and performed a principal component analysis (PCA) on consumer survey data. I find significant changes in the long-run relationship between consumption and its determinants, driven by income and financial wealth variables.
